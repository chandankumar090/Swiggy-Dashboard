# Swiggy Sales Analytics Dashboard 📊📱

A comprehensive, data-driven **Excel Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze and visualize sales performance, customer behavior, and regional trends for Swiggy. This project transforms raw operational data into actionable business insights, helping stakeholders monitor key metrics, track growth patterns, and optimize strategic decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard provides a high-level summary of business health across **five core metrics**:
* **Total Sales:** ₹53.01M in revenue generated.
* **Average Rating:** 4.34 out of 5 stars, indicating strong overall customer satisfaction.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹268.51 spent per order.
* **Rating Count:** 5.59M total reviews submitted.
* **Total Orders:** 197.43K successful orders processed.

---

## 📈 Visualizations & Features
The dashboard incorporates diverse data visualization techniques to capture multi-dimensional business trends:

* **Sales Distribution by Food Type:** A breakdown showing consumer preference, highlighting that **Non-Veg items account for 64%** (34.2M) of sales, while **Veg items make up 36%** (18.8M).
* **Geographical Sales Performance:** An interactive filled map of India showcasing regional revenue distribution, identifying high-performing states like Karnataka.
* **Temporal Trend Analysis:**
  * *Monthly Sales Trend:* Line chart monitoring revenue fluctuations from January to August.
  * *Weekly Sales Trend:* Granular column chart tracking consistent sales performance over a 36-week span.
  * *Daily Sales Trend:* Bar chart revealing weekly ordering habits, peaking on Saturdays at **7.8M**.
* **Top 5 Cities by Sales:** A horizontal bar chart identifying top revenue-generating urban centers, led by **Bengaluru (₹5.46M)**, followed by Lucknow, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and New Delhi.
* **Quarterly Breakdowns:** A clean tabular matrix tracking Sales, Average Rating, and Order Counts across Q1, Q2, and Q3.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)
To allow users to easily drill down into specific data points, the dashboard includes dynamic slicers for:
* **Months:** Filter data instantly from January through September.
* **Category:** Segment insights by food types (e.g., Pasta, Rolls, Chinese, Sides).
* **Restaurant Name:** Analyze individual performance across specific major chains and local eateries (e.g., The Hocco Kitchen, The Central Restaurant).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
* **Data Modeling:** Power Query (for ETL processing and cleaning raw datasets).
* **Analytics Engine:** Pivot Tables and advanced Excel formulas (`SUMIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `VLOOKUP`).
* **UI/UX Design:** Conditional formatting, cohesive color palette integration, custom KPI cards, and dynamic cross-filtering slicers.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights Derived
1. **Weekend Surge:** Sales significantly peak over the weekend (Friday–Saturday), suggesting a prime window for targeted marketing campaigns and push notifications.
2. **Dominant Market:** Bengaluru is the single largest revenue hub for the platform within this dataset, making it a critical baseline for testing new features or loyalty programs.
3. **Dietary Preference:** Non-veg dishes heavily drive gross merchandise value (GMV), capturing nearly two-thirds of the total market share.
